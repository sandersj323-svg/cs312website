Josh's Chess Tutorial

This website will be developed throughout the semester as part of CS 312. My finished website will focus on my proposed topic, which is chess. I will be creating a tutorial for beginners to learn how to play chess, as well as some strategies and tips for improving their game. I also plan on including some interactive features, such as a chess board where users can practice their moves and a quiz to test their knowledge of chess rules and strategies. I am excited to work on this project and share my knowledge of chess with others.
